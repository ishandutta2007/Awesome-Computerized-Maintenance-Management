# Awesome-Computerized-Maintenance-Management

## Top Computerized Maintenance Management (CMMS) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Work Orders, Preventive Maintenance, Asset Management, Inventory, Mobile Maintenance & Facility Operations*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Computerized Maintenance Management Systems (CMMS)**. These systems help maintenance teams manage assets, work orders, preventive maintenance schedules, parts inventory, and field technician workflows.



**Examples** include UpKeep, MaintainX, Fiix, Limble CMMS, eMaint, Hippo CMMS, FMX, MicroMain, ManagerPlus, MPulse, Fracttal, and Asset Panda (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Modern mobile-first CMMS is largely commercial, but solid open options exist—especially **openMAINT** (built on CMDBuild) and related asset/maintenance platforms. This section expands those projects while remaining realistic about mobile UX and ease-of-use gaps versus leading SaaS.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[UpKeep](https://www.upkeep.com/)**  

  Mobile-first CMMS for work orders, asset management, preventive maintenance, and inventory—popular with industrial and facility teams.



- **[MaintainX](https://www.getmaintainx.com/)**  

  Leading modern CMMS focused on work orders, preventive maintenance, parts, and strong mobile experience for frontline technicians.



- **[Fiix](https://www.fiixsoftware.com/)**  

  Cloud CMMS (Rockwell Automation) covering assets, work orders, inventory, and scheduling, with a free tier and enterprise options.



- **[Limble CMMS](https://limble.com/)**  

  Fast-to-deploy CMMS for work orders, PM, and asset tracking aimed at mid-market maintenance teams replacing legacy systems.



- **[eMaint](https://www.emaint.com/)**  

  CMMS with strong condition-based and reliability features, often connected to Fluke and industrial monitoring ecosystems.



- **[Hippo CMMS](https://www.hippocmms.com/)**  

  Maintenance management software for work orders, preventive maintenance, and facility operations.



- **[FMX](https://www.gofmx.com/)**  

  Facility management and CMMS platform used by schools, municipalities, and organizations for work requests and maintenance.



- **[MicroMain](https://www.micromain.com/)**  

  CMMS for asset and maintenance management across industrial and facility environments.



- **[ManagerPlus](https://www.managerplus.com/)**  

  Asset and maintenance management software (often associated with fleet and facility use cases).



- **[MPulse](https://www.mpulsesoftware.com/)**  

  CMMS focused on work orders, preventive maintenance, and asset performance for maintenance departments.



- **[Fracttal](https://www.fracttal.com/)**  

  Cloud CMMS/EAM platform for asset management, work orders, and maintenance operations.



- **[Asset Panda](https://www.assetpanda.com/)**  

  Flexible asset tracking and management platform often used alongside or as a lighter alternative to full CMMS workflows.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[openMAINT](https://www.openmaint.org/)**  

  Open-source CMMS for mobile assets, plants, technical devices, and facility maintenance—scheduled and breakdown work, logistics, and economic tracking (based on CMDBuild).



- **[CMDBuild](https://www.cmdbuild.org/)**  

  Open-source platform for configuring asset management and CMDB applications; foundation for openMAINT and custom maintenance solutions.



- **[SuperCMMS / open CMMS backends](https://github.com/)**  

  Community open-source CMMS backend efforts covering assets, work orders, preventive maintenance, checklists, QR codes, and inventory.



- **[Odoo Maintenance modules](https://github.com/odoo)**  

  Open-source ERP maintenance apps for equipment, work orders, and preventive maintenance within the Odoo ecosystem.



- **[GLPI plugins for maintenance](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi)**  

  Open-source IT asset and service management platform that can be extended for facility and equipment maintenance workflows.



- **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)**  

  Open-source IT asset management useful for tracking equipment lifecycle; complementary to full CMMS work-order processes.



- **[Community CMMS and work-order open projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Smaller self-hosted work-order and asset tracking applications for local facilities and plants.



- **[PostgreSQL + open reporting for maintenance KPIs](https://www.postgresql.org/)**  

  Open database and BI stacks used to build custom maintenance dashboards on top of CMMS data.



- **[Mobile work-order open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental open mobile apps for technicians to receive and close work orders.



- **[Documentation and openMAINT / CMDBuild playbooks](https://www.openmaint.org/en)**  

  Guides for deploying open-source CMMS in facilities, industrial sites, and public-sector environments.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **openMAINT** for facility and asset-centric maintenance with full open-source control.

- Extending **CMDBuild** when highly customized asset models and workflows are required.

- Using **Odoo Maintenance** when CMMS needs to sit inside a broader open ERP.

- Accepting that polished mobile technician apps, rapid SaaS onboarding, native IoT/condition integrations, and large multi-site support still favor commercial CMMS (MaintainX, UpKeep, Fiix, Limble, eMaint, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, cost control, and deep facility/asset modeling.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Model assets and locations in openMAINT/CMDBuild → create work orders and PM schedules → track parts and labor → report KPIs via open BI. Suitable for facilities teams, municipalities, and industrial sites with technical capacity. Many organizations still choose commercial CMMS for mobile UX and speed of adoption.
